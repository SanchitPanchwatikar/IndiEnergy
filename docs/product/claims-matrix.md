# AuraSync Claims Matrix

> **Document control:** Version 0.1 | Status: Draft — no product claims approved | Owner: Founder / product owner (TBD) | Last reviewed: 2026-11-20

This is a review register, not permission to publish. A claim becomes **Approved** only after evidence, product/legal review for the chosen market, an accountable approver, and a review date are recorded. Until then, use only factual descriptions of verified prototype functionality and clearly label the product as a concept or prototype.

## Status definitions

- **Proposed:** candidate wording; not approved.
- **Evidence needed:** technically or empirically unverified.
- **Review required:** evidence exists or is planned, but qualified review/approval is missing.
- **Approved:** evidence and required reviewers/owner are recorded for a specific market and channel.
- **Prohibited:** do not use.

## Approval record requirements

For each claim approved, record:

1. Exact wording, channel, audience, language, and target market.
2. Evidence source, date, limitations, and whether it supports that exact wording.
3. Device/app version and performance conditions, if applicable.
4. Required disclaimer or qualification and how it is displayed.
5. Founder and qualified reviewer names, approval date, and re-review trigger.

Re-review claims when functionality, sensor, evidence, audience, market, or advertising channel changes. Do not convert exploratory pilot outcomes into efficacy claims without appropriate review.
